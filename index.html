Meet KRU

The first AI-native companion for athletes, supporting body and mind.

Available 24/7, KRU learns your goals, routines and progress through everyday conversations, giving personalised guidance across training, nutrition, recovery and mental wellbeing. When specialised care is needed, it connects you with qualified practitioners and keeps your coaches and parents in the loop.
