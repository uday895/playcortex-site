What is KRU?+

KRU is a smart companion for sport, pairing AI with human expertise. It helps with your training, rest, food and mood, and connects you to real experts when you need them.

Does KRU work on WhatsApp and iMessage?+

Yes. You can chat with KRU on WhatsApp, iMessage or the KRU app.

Is KRU a doctor or a physio?+

No. KRU is AI, and it can make mistakes. It does not give medical advice. For anything medical, KRU sends you to a qualified expert.

Who are the experts?+

Strength coaches, sports psychologists, nutritionists and physios from India. KRU books the call and shares your report with them.

What do my coach and parents see?+

How you are doing and what you need to work on. Your private chats stay between you and KRU.

Can under-18s use KRU?+

Yes, if a parent or guardian agrees first.

How do I get KRU?+

KRU is being tested with partner academies right now. Join the waitlist and we will contact you when it is your turn.
