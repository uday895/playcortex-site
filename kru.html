Top athletes have experts behind their body and mind. Most aspiring athletes have to find and coordinate that support alone. KRU is the AI-native companion that changes this: available 24/7, supporting body and mind, with real experts when it matters.

How it works

Talk to KRU on the app or WhatsApp, by voice or text, any time. Through everyday conversations it learns your goals, routines and progress, and gives personalised guidance for body and mind: training, nutrition, recovery and mental wellbeing.

Lift like the coach in front of you

Film a set on your phone. KRU tracks 33 points on your body, measures every rep against an expert's demo and tells you what to fix before the next one.

  1. Expert demoEach movement starts from a practitioner's reference, like this trap bar deadlift from Future Self Performance Institute.
  2. Real-time trackingDots lock onto your shoulders, hips, knees and ankles and follow every rep.
  3. Cues, not jargonHip hinge, back angle and lockout, scored rep by rep with one cue at a time.
  4. Expert in the loopAnything off gets flagged to your S&C coach, with the clip and the numbers attached.
