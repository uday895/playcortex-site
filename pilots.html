How the pilots work

Who takes part

Athletes at our partner academies, with their coaches. Our physios, strength coaches and sports psychologists guide each plan.

The first test

On day one, our field team runs four simple tests at the ground: push-ups, a side plank on each side, a shoulder reach test and an overhead squat.

How we measure

We count the reps, time the holds and photograph each test. Pose tracking then marks the joints and measures the angles, like how deep the squat goes.

A report for each athlete

Each athlete gets a short report with their results, the photos and what we noticed. Coaches and experts use it to set the plan.

Every day with KRU

For the next few weeks, athletes check in, train with their plan and chat with KRU whenever they need help.

The second test

At the end, we run the same tests again and compare the two reports, so everyone can see what changed.

What we measure

Six simple things. Each one answers one question about KRU.

Daily check-ins

How many days each athlete told KRU how they slept, felt and trained.

Shows if the habit sticks.

Chats with KRU

How often athletes asked KRU for help with training, food or nerves.

Shows if they find it useful.

Sessions done

How many planned sessions were finished, on time or late.

Shows if athletes follow their plan.

Body tests

Simple tests at the start and end, like push-ups, squat depth and balance on one leg.

Shows if they got stronger and move better.

Mood and focus

Daily mood check-ins, plus notes from the sports psychologist.

Shows if they feel calmer and more confident.

Expert changes

How often a physio or coach changed a plan because of something KRU flagged.

Shows KRU is catching the right things.
