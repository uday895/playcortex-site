Write it once

Your playbook, in plain language

No forms, no code. You tell KRU how you work, as rules like these. It follows them in every chat and plan, and never goes outside them.

IF

lockout under 150° on a trap bar set

THEN

cue “stand tall”, drop the load 10%, flag me if it repeats

WHEN

an under-19 trial is 6 weeks out

THEN

peak strength in weeks 1 to 4, taper in weeks 5 and 6

IF

sleep under 6 hours two nights in a row

THEN

swap the heavy day for mobility

IF

shoulder soreness 4 out of 10 or more for 2 days

THEN

no bowling, band work only, send me the athlete

IF

pain gets worse during a session

THEN

stop the session and book a check with me

WHEN

an athlete returns from injury

THEN

follow my 4-stage return to bowling, I approve each stage

WHEN

a match starts before 10 am

THEN

breakfast 3 hours before, a light snack 1 hour before

IF

training load jumps more than 30% in a week

THEN

add a recovery meal and remind them at 9 pm

IF

an athlete asks about supplements

THEN

only suggest from my approved list

IF

“nervous” or “can’t sleep” the night before selection

THEN

a 2-minute breathing reset and one cue for tomorrow

WHEN

three low-mood check-ins land in a week

THEN

flag me and suggest a session

IF

anything about self-harm comes up

THEN

stop, show helplines and alert the safeguarding lead
