KRU for experts

You are a strength and conditioning coach, physio, nutritionist or sport psychologist. KRU takes your method to many more athletes than you could see one by one. It runs the day-to-day check-ins and plans within the direction you set, and brings you in when an athlete needs your expertise.

Apply to join ›See the expert view ›

How it works

Step 1

Join the network

Apply and have a short call with our team. We learn how you work, what you assess and the outcomes you care about.

Step 2

You set the direction

Your assessments, your goals and your rules. KRU follows them when it builds and adjusts each athlete's plan, and never goes outside them.

Step 3

Step in where it counts

KRU handles the daily check-ins and flags the athletes who need you. You review, approve or take over, and spend your time where it makes a difference.

Every athlete who needs you, in one place

See each referred athlete's training load, recovery, wellbeing and match workload next to the coach's goal. KRU explains why it flagged them and drafts a next step for you to approve, edit or replace.
