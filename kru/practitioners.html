KRU for practitioners

KRU follows your advice with athletes every day, and sends them to you when they need you.

Apply to joinSee how it works

Who it is for

Physios

KRU collects pain reports, keeps athletes on your rehab steps, and sends you a case with a full pain report when someone needs you.

Strength and conditioning coaches

KRU runs your programmes, checks form on video, and changes the load when an athlete’s sleep or pain says so.

Nutritionists

KRU reminds athletes what to eat around training and matches, checks meal photos, and only suggests what you approve.

Sports psychologists

KRU checks mood every day, runs your breathing and focus exercises, and tells you about low moods or anything serious.

How it works

You, KRU and your athletes work in a loop. Follow the numbers.

What you can do

Everything a practitioner gets with KRU.

Write your rules

Tell KRU how you treat common problems, in plain words. For example: if a bowler’s knee pain is 3 out of 10 or more, no nets for 2 days.

Test before it goes live

See what a rule would have done over the last 30 days, and who it would have affected, before you switch it on.

KRU follows your rules daily

Every check-in and chat uses your advice. KRU never goes past your rules, and anything medical comes to you.

Get cases

When an athlete needs you, they show up in your cases with what they said and what they agreed to share.

Read pain reports

A ready report for each case: where it hurts, how bad, since when and what KRU did. In English or Hindi, as a PDF.

See data reports

An athlete’s last 4 weeks of sleep, training, mood, form and bowling, on one page.

Follow up from your phone

Accept cases, write what you did, and say no to or undo anything KRU changed.

Private by default

You only see your own cases, and only what the athlete chose to share. Under-18s join with a parent’s consent.

What you see

Your cases, and the two reports KRU writes for every case.

My cases

Every athlete who needs you shows up here. One look tells you who they are, what they said, if a parent agreed, and anything they asked you not to do.

Examples of rules

Each rule says: when this happens, do this. Pick your field to see examples, and what KRU does with each one.

You tell KRU

“If a kid’s pain is 3 out of 10 or more, no nets for 2 days, only stretching, and tell me.”

What happens

Ojas says his knee hurts when he lands after bowling, 3 out of 10. KRU stops his nets for 2 days, gives him stretches and sends you a case.

You tell KRU

“If a bowler’s shoulder pain is 4 out of 10 or more for 2 days, stop his bowling, give him band exercises only, and send him to me.”

What happens

Mayank says his shoulder pain is 5 out of 10 on Tuesday and again on Wednesday. KRU stops his bowling, sends him band exercises and books him with you.

You tell KRU

“If pain gets worse during a session, stop the session and book a check-up with me.”

What happens

Halfway through nets, Ojas says his knee hurts more. KRU tells him to stop and books him a check-up with you.

You tell KRU

“If an athlete sleeps less than 6 hours two nights in a row, swap the heavy gym day for stretching.”

What happens

Vihaan sleeps 5 hours on Sunday and Monday. On Tuesday, KRU gives him stretching instead of heavy weights.

You tell KRU

“If a big trial is 6 weeks away, build strength for 4 weeks, then keep the last 2 weeks light.”

What happens

Tanvi has a trial on 1 March. KRU plans 4 strong weeks, then 2 light weeks before the trial.

You tell KRU

“If a match starts before 10 am, tell them to eat breakfast 3 hours before and a small snack 1 hour before.”

What happens

Kunal has a 9 am match. At 6 am KRU reminds him to eat breakfast, and at 8 am, a banana.

You tell KRU

“If an athlete asks about supplements, only suggest the ones on my list.”

What happens

Parth asks KRU about protein powder. KRU only suggests the ones you have approved.

You tell KRU

“If an athlete says they are nervous or cannot sleep before selection, do a 2-minute breathing exercise with them.”

What happens

The night before selection, Nikhil tells KRU he cannot sleep. KRU does a 2-minute breathing exercise with him.

You tell KRU

“If an athlete has a low mood 3 times in one week, tell me and suggest a session.”

What happens

Tanvi has a low mood on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. KRU tells you and offers her a session with you.
