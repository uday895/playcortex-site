KRU for academies

For coaches and academies, KRU brings each athlete's plans, feedback and progress into one clear view, helping them track development, spot emerging concerns and make informed decisions about training and support.

Contact us ›See the coach view ›

How it works

Step 1

Onboard your academy

We set up your squad on KRU. Each athlete gets the app, and you get one coach view for the whole group.

Step 2

Athletes check in

Every day, athletes tell KRU about training, food, sleep and how they feel, by voice or text, in the app or on WhatsApp.

Step 3

You see everything

KRU turns check-ins and match scores into one view per athlete, and flags anyone who needs you or a practitioner.

The full picture of every athlete

Bring each athlete's training, nutrition, recovery, mental wellbeing and match performance into one connected view. See how they're developing over time, understand how physical strain and wellbeing may be affecting performance, and identify who needs closer attention. With plans, feedback and progress in one place, coaches can make more informed decisions and involve the right practitioner when additional support is needed.
