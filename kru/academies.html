Ask KRU

Ask in Hindi or English

Type or send a voice note, in Hindi or English. KRU looks at every kid’s data and answers in seconds.

  • “Vivian ne is hafte kya kiya aur kya miss kiya?”
  • Voice notes turn into tasks you confirm
  • Nothing goes to a kid until you say yes

How to start

Step 1

We set you up

We add your squad to KRU. Each kid gets the app. You get the coach app and the coach workspace.

Step 2

Kids check in

Every day, kids check in, train and talk to KRU about training, food, sleep and how they feel.

Step 3

You see everything

KRU puts it all on one page for each kid, and tells you who needs you or a physio.

Every kid

Each kid’s full picture

Each kid’s sessions, check-ins, pain and plan, in one place. If a kid needs a physio, a report is ready to send.

  • The whole plan, week by week
  • Pain reports as a clean PDF
  • Check and undo anything KRU changes
