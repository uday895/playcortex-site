What KRU does

KRU is an AI companion that brings an athlete's training, nutrition, recovery and mental wellbeing into one connected support system. Developed with leading sports practitioners, it understands your goals, history and daily training through conversation, providing personalised guidance that evolves with you. When professional care is needed, KRU connects you with the right practitioner and coordinates the next steps.
