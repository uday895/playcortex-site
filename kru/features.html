KRU for athletes

One app to check in, train, eat well and talk things through. Built with your coach and top sports experts.

Get early access
01

Talk to KRU

Ask KRU anything about your game, your body or your mind. Type or send a voice note. KRU replies in English, Hindi or Hinglish and tells you what to do next.

  • Type or talk, the way you like
  • Fitness and mind coaches in one chat
  • Start a session in one tap
02

Body guide

Does something hurt? Tap the spot on the body picture. KRU asks a few simple questions and tells your coach. KRU is not a doctor, but it makes sure the right person knows.

  • Tap where it hurts
  • See your pain get better, day by day
  • Your coach knows straight away
03 · New

Check your form

Put your phone down and film one set. KRU watches your body and checks every rep, the way your coach taught you. Then it tells you one thing to fix.

  • Your coach decides what good form looks like
  • KRU follows your shoulders, hips, knees and ankles
  • One simple tip after each set
  • If something looks wrong, your coach sees the video
04 · New

Snap your meal

Take a photo of your plate. KRU tells you what is in it and if it is enough for your training today. The tips use food you already eat at home.

  • Works with Indian home food
  • Shows protein, carbs and vegetables
  • One easy tip for your next meal

05

Your workout plan

KRU makes a plan for your goal, and your coach checks it. Every session shows you what to do, step by step, with videos. After a few weeks, you test again to see how much better you got.

  • A plan made for your goal
  • Videos for every exercise
  • A test to see your progress
06

Top experts from India

When you need a real person, KRU sends you to a physio, doctor, nutritionist or sports psychologist. It writes a short report for them, so you do not have to explain everything again.

  • A report ready for your physio or doctor
  • Messages from your coach and experts in one place
  • You choose what is shared
  • Under 18? A parent or guardian agrees first
07

Cricket games

Quick cricket games that train your decisions: run or stay, leave or play, where to hit the gap. Your scores show how sharp you are over time.

  • 9 games for speed, timing and decisions
  • A new game every day
  • See your scores go up
08

Points and streaks

You earn points for check-ins, sessions and games. Keep your streak going and climb your academy’s weekly leaderboard.

  • Weekly leaderboards with your academy
  • Streaks for showing up every day
  • Badges when you reach a goal
