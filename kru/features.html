What KRU does

KRU is the AI-native companion for athletes, supporting body and mind. Available 24/7 and built with leading sports practitioners, it learns your goals, routines and progress through everyday conversations, and gives personalised guidance across training, nutrition, recovery and mental wellbeing that evolves with you. When specialised care is needed, it connects you with qualified practitioners and coordinates the next steps.
